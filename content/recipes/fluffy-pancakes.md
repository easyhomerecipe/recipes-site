  - "1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour"

  - "2 tsp baking powder"
  - "1/2 tsp salt"
  - "1 1/4 cups milk"
  - "1 large egg"
  - "3 tbsp melted butter, plus more for the pan"
  - "1 tsp vanilla extract"
steps:
  - "In a large bowl, whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt."
  - "In another bowl, whisk the milk, egg, melted butter and vanilla."
  - "Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir gently until just combined. Small lumps are fine."
  - "Let the batter rest for 5 minutes while you heat a lightly buttered pan over medium heat."
  - "Pour 1/4 cup of batter per pancake. Cook until bubbles form on top, about 2 minutes, then flip and cook 1–2 minutes more."
  - "Serve warm with maple syrup, honey or fresh fruit."

These fluffy pancakes are our go-to weekend breakfast. They are thick, soft and golden, and you only need basic pantry ingredients and 20 minutes.

## The secret to fluffy pancakes

The key is to **mix gently** and **let the batter rest** for a few minutes. This gives the baking powder time to work and creates those airy bubbles.

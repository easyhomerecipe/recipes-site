ingredients:
  - "4 tablespoons all-purpose flour (30 g)"
  - "4 tablespoons granulated sugar (50 g)"
  - "2 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder (15 g)"
  - "1/8 teaspoon baking powder"
  - "1/8 teaspoon salt"
  - "3 tablespoons milk (45 ml)"
  - "2 tablespoons vegetable oil (30 ml)"
  - "1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract"
  - "2 tablespoons chocolate chips (30 g), optional"
steps:
  - "In a microwave-safe mug, combine the flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder, and salt."
  - "Stir in the milk, vegetable oil, and vanilla extract until smooth."
  - "Fold in the chocolate chips, if using."
  - "Microwave the mug on high for 60-90 seconds, watching carefully to avoid overflow."
  - "Remove from the microwave and let it cool for a minute."
  - "Enjoy your mug cake warm, directly from the mug!"

## Deliciously Simple Fudgy Mug Cake
Craving something sweet but don’t want to spend hours in the kitchen? This fudgy microwave chocolate mug cake is here to save the day! It’s quick, easy, and requires no eggs, making it perfect for a last-minute dessert. Made in a mug and microwaved in just 5 minutes, you’ll have a warm, chocolatey treat ready to enjoy in no time. Perfect for chilly winter evenings or cozy holiday gatherings!

## The Perfect Treat Anytime
This recipe is not only super simple, but it’s also highly customizable. Whether it’s a last-minute dessert for guests or a midnight snack, you can whip this up with ingredients you probably already have at home. Feel free to add toppings like whipped cream, ice cream, or even fresh berries to elevate your treat to the next level. Plus, this mug cake is perfect for those holiday gatherings where everyone wants to enjoy a little something sweet without fussing over the oven.

No need for elaborate baking sheets or pans – just grab your favorite mug and let the microwave do the work. Enjoy your fudgy chocolate mug cake with a cup of hot cocoa or tea for a complete cozy treat this winter season!

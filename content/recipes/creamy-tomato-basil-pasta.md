ingredients:
  - "12 oz (340 g) penne pasta"
  - "2 tbsp (30 g) olive oil"
  - "3 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "1 can (28 oz / 800 g) crushed tomatoes"
  - "1/2 cup (120 ml) heavy cream"
  - "1 cup (30 g) fresh basil leaves, chopped"
  - "1 tsp (5 g) sugar"
  - "1/2 tsp (2 g) red pepper flakes"
  - "Salt and pepper to taste"
  - "Grated Parmesan cheese for serving"
steps:
  - "Cook the penne pasta according to package instructions in salted water."
  - "In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat."
  - "Add minced garlic and sauté until fragrant, about 1 minute."
  - "Pour in the crushed tomatoes and stir well."
  - "Add heavy cream, sugar, red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper; mix until combined."
  - "Simmer the sauce for about 10 minutes, allowing it to thicken slightly."
  - "Drain the pasta and add it to the skillet with the sauce."
  - "Stir in the fresh basil, mixing until the pasta is well coated."
  - "Serve immediately, topped with grated Parmesan cheese."
tips:
  - "For added flavor, use San Marzano tomatoes if available."
  - "Adjust the spice level by increasing or decreasing the red pepper flakes."
  - "Add cooked chicken or shrimp for extra protein."

## Introduction
Pasta lovers, rejoice! This Creamy Tomato Basil Pasta is a delightful combination of flavors that will warm your heart and satisfy your cravings. Perfect for weekdays or weekend dining, this dish can be prepared in under 30 minutes and is sure to impress your family or guests.

## Simple Ingredients for a Delicious Meal
The beauty of this dish lies in its simplicity. With just a handful of ingredients including penne pasta, crushed tomatoes, heavy cream, and fresh basil, each component works harmoniously to create a rich and comforting sauce. The addition of garlic and a hint of red pepper flakes adds a gentle kick, enhancing the overall flavor profile. Plus, it’s a great way to use up any fresh basil you might have left from your garden or market haul.

## Cooking Tips and Variations
Cooking the pasta al dente before adding it to the sauce ensures that it holds its shape and texture. Adjust the creaminess by simply adding more or less heavy cream to suit your personal taste. This dish also allows for creativity — consider adding sautéed vegetables like zucchini or bell peppers, or toss in cooked chicken or shrimp for a protein-packed version. When serving, don’t forget the grated Parmesan on top for that final touch!

Whether you’re looking for a quick dinner idea or a dish to impress, Creamy Tomato Basil Pasta is a fantastic choice. Enjoy every bite!

ingredients:
  - "4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (680g)"
  - "1 pound (450g) fresh asparagus, trimmed"
  - "2 tablespoons olive oil (30ml)"
  - "1 lemon, zested and juiced"
  - "3 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "1 teaspoon dried thyme (1g)"
  - "1 teaspoon dried rosemary (1g)"
  - "Salt and pepper to taste"
  - "1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional, 1g)"
  - "Fresh parsley for garnish (optional)"
steps:
  - "Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C)."
  - "In a large bowl, combine olive oil, lemon juice, lemon zest, garlic, thyme, rosemary, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes."
  - "Add the chicken breasts to the bowl and toss to coat evenly."
  - "Heat a large oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat and add the chicken breasts."
  - "Sear the chicken for 3-4 minutes on each side until golden brown."
  - "Add the trimmed asparagus around the chicken in the skillet."
  - "Drizzle remaining marinade over the asparagus."
  - "Transfer the skillet to the preheated oven and bake for 15-20 minutes until the chicken is cooked through."
  - "Garnish with fresh parsley and serve warm."
tips:
  - "For extra flavor, marinate the chicken for at least an hour before cooking."
  - "Make sure to trim the asparagus to ensure even cooking."
  - "This recipe can be easily doubled for larger gatherings."

## A Flavorful Weeknight Delight

If you’re looking for a quick and delicious weeknight dinner, look no further! This Easy One-Pan Lemon Herb Chicken and Asparagus is not only simple to prepare but also packed with flavor. Using fresh ingredients, this dish can be whipped up in just under an hour, making it perfect for busy evenings. The combination of lemon, herbs, and garlic creates a wonderful aroma that will fill your kitchen, making it hard to resist! 

## Cooking Made Easy

One of the best things about this recipe is how little cleanup is needed. With just one pan, you can cook both your protein and veggies. This allows the flavors to meld together beautifully, giving you a wholesome meal without the hassle of multiple dishes. Plus, the asparagus adds a vibrant color and crunch, making it a visually appealing plate. It’s a great way to sneak in some greens for the family! 

## Serving Suggestions

To elevate your meal, consider serving this chicken and asparagus with a side of rice or a simple green salad. You can also adjust the level of garlic or herbs to suit your taste. This dish embodies everything a good weeknight dinner should be: delicious, satisfying, and effortless. So, grab your apron and get cooking—you'll be enjoying this delightful meal in no time!

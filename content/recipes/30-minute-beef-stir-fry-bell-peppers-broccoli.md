ingredients:
  - "1 lb (450g) flank steak, thinly sliced"
  - "2 tablespoons (30ml) soy sauce"
  - "1 tablespoon (15ml) sesame oil"
  - "1 tablespoon (15g) cornstarch"
  - "1 red bell pepper, sliced"
  - "1 yellow bell pepper, sliced"
  - "2 cups (150g) broccoli florets"
  - "2 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "1 tablespoon (15ml) vegetable oil"
  - "1 tablespoon (15ml) oyster sauce"
  - "1 teaspoon (5g) ginger, grated"
  - "1/4 cup (60ml) beef broth"
  - "Cooked rice or noodles for serving"
steps:
  - "In a bowl, mix the sliced beef with soy sauce, sesame oil, and cornstarch; set aside for 10 minutes."
  - "Heat vegetable oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat."
  - "Add the marinated beef to the skillet, stirring frequently for 3-4 minutes until browned."
  - "Remove the beef from the skillet and set aside."
  - "In the same skillet, add garlic, ginger, and bell peppers; stir-fry for 2-3 minutes."
  - "Add broccoli florets and stir-fry for another 3 minutes until tender-crisp."
  - "Return the beef to the skillet; pour in beef broth and oyster sauce, stir to combine."
  - "Cook for an additional 2 minutes until everything is heated through."
  - "Serve hot over cooked rice or noodles."
tips:
  - "Slice the beef against the grain for more tenderness."
  - "Prep all your ingredients before cooking to speed up the process."
  - "Feel free to add other vegetables like snap peas or carrots for more color and nutrients."

## Quick and Delicious Beef Stir-Fry

When you’re short on time but still want a hearty meal, this 30-minute beef stir-fry is just what you need. Bursting with flavors and colorful vegetables, this dish is not only delicious but also incredibly easy to make. With flank steak, bell peppers, and broccoli, it’s a quick dinner option that will satisfy your family and impress your friends.

## Why You'll Love This Recipe

The best part about this beef stir-fry is how customizable it is. Feel free to use whatever veggies you have on hand. Snap peas, carrots, or even mushrooms can all work well in this dish. Plus, it’s a great way to use up leftovers in your fridge. Just whip up some rice or noodles to serve it over, and you’ve got a meal that’s both nutritious and filling. 

## Cooking Tips

To make the stir-frying process even smoother, be sure to prep your ingredients before you start cooking. Slice your veggies and beef ahead of time, and have everything within reach. This not only saves time but ensures that the cooking process is quick and efficient. Enjoy this beef stir-fry on busy weeknights and make it a staple in your dinner rotation!

ingredients:
  - "1 pound (450 g) large shrimp, peeled and deveined"
  - "1 cup (200 g) jasmine rice"
  - "2 cups (480 ml) low-sodium chicken broth"
  - "4 tablespoons (60 g) unsalted butter"
  - "4 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "1 teaspoon paprika"
  - "1 cup (100 g) grated Parmesan cheese"
  - "1 cup (150 g) cherry tomatoes, halved"
  - "1/2 cup (75 g) frozen peas"
  - "Salt and pepper to taste"
  - "2 tablespoons (30 ml) chopped fresh parsley"
steps:
  - "In a large skillet, melt 2 tablespoons of butter over medium heat."
  - "Add minced garlic and sauté for about 1 minute until fragrant."
  - "Stir in the jasmine rice and cook for 1-2 minutes to toast."
  - "Pour in the chicken broth and bring to a boil."
  - "Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes until rice is tender."
  - "Add the shrimp, cherry tomatoes, frozen peas, and remaining butter to the skillet."
  - "Sprinkle with paprika, salt, and pepper, and cook for another 5-7 minutes until shrimp are pink and cooked through (minimum 165°F / 74°C)."
  - "Stir in the Parmesan cheese until melted and creamy."
  - "Garnish with fresh parsley before serving."
tips:
  - "Use thawed shrimp for better texture; if using frozen, ensure they are fully thawed before cooking."
  - "Feel free to add your favorite vegetables like bell peppers or spinach for added nutrition."
  - "For extra flavor, consider adding a splash of lemon juice just before serving."
faq:
  - q: "Can I use brown rice instead of jasmine rice?"
    a: "Yes, but you will need to adjust the cooking time and increase the liquid to about 2 1/2 cups."
  - q: "How can I make this dish spicier?"
    a: "Add red pepper flakes or diced jalapeños when you sauté the garlic."
  - q: "What can I substitute for shrimp?"
    a: "You can use chicken breast or firm tofu, cooking them until fully cooked (chicken at 165°F / 74°C)."

## One-Pan Garlic Parmesan Shrimp and Rice

One-pan meals are a lifesaver when it comes to busy weeknights. They’re quick, easy to clean up, and still deliver great flavor. This One-Pan Garlic Parmesan Shrimp and Rice combines succulent shrimp with aromatic garlic and creamy Parmesan cheese, making for a satisfying dinner option that the whole family will love.

### The Perfect Balance of Flavors
This dish is not only delicious but also versatile. By using ingredients that are likely already in your pantry, you can whip up this meal in about thirty minutes. The key ingredients—shrimp, jasmine rice, garlic, and Parmesan—come together beautifully, creating a rich and savory flavor profile that pairs excellently with a side salad or some crusty bread.

### Quick and Easy Cooking
One of the best things about this recipe is how straightforward it is. Everything cooks in one pan, which means you spend less time doing dishes and more time enjoying your meal. In just ten minutes of prep and twenty minutes of cooking, you’ll have a hearty dish ready to serve. Perfect for a weeknight dinner or a relaxed weekend meal with loved ones, this shrimp and rice combo is sure to be a hit at your dinner table!

ingredients:
  - "1 lb (450g) ground beef"
  - "1 tablespoon (15g) olive oil"
  - "1 small onion, diced"
  - "2 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "1 tablespoon (15g) taco seasoning"
  - "1/2 cup (120ml) beef broth"
  - "8 small corn tortillas"
  - "1 cup (100g) shredded lettuce"
  - "1 cup (150g) diced tomatoes"
  - "1/2 cup (120g) shredded cheddar cheese"
  - "1/4 cup (60g) sour cream"
  - "1/4 cup (60g) salsa"
steps:
  - "Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat."
  - "Add diced onion and sauté until translucent, about 3 minutes."
  - "Stir in minced garlic and cook for an additional 1 minute."
  - "Add ground beef to the skillet, breaking it up with a spoon."
  - "Cook until beef is browned and cooked through, about 5-7 minutes."
  - "Sprinkle taco seasoning over the beef and stir to combine."
  - "Pour in beef broth and simmer for 5 minutes until the mixture thickens."
  - "While the beef is cooking, warm the corn tortillas in a separate pan for 1-2 minutes each side."
  - "Assemble tacos by placing beef mixture in tortillas and topping with lettuce, tomatoes, cheese, sour cream, and salsa."
  - "Serve immediately and enjoy your homemade beef tacos!"
tips:
  - "For extra flavor, let the beef mixture simmer a little longer to deepen the taste."
  - "Feel free to customize your toppings with avocado, jalapeños, or hot sauce."
  - "Make it a taco bar by serving all toppings separately for a fun family dinner."

## Delicious and Quick Beef Tacos
Tacos are one of the most versatile and loved dishes around the world, and making them at home can be a breeze! If you're looking for a tasty meal that can be ready in just 30 minutes, this classic beef taco recipe is perfect for you. Perfect for busy weeknights or casual gatherings, these homemade tacos will surely please your family and friends.

## Simple Ingredients
With just a handful of ingredients that you likely already have in your pantry, making these tacos is as easy as 1-2-3. Ground beef forms the base of the taco filling, while a simple blend of onions, garlic, and taco seasoning brings in the flavors we all love. Add in your favorite toppings like fresh lettuce, diced tomatoes, and shredded cheese, and you'll have a delicious, satisfying meal.

## Customize to Your Taste
One of the best parts about tacos is that you can personalize them to your liking. Want to spice things up? Add some jalapeños or hot sauce for a kick. Prefer something milder? Stick with the classics! Plus, these tacos are great for family dinners, where everyone can build their own tacos just the way they like them. Whether it's a busy weeknight or a fun weekend gathering, these beef tacos are sure to become a favorite in your home.

ingredients:
  - "2 tablespoons olive oil"
  - "1 onion, chopped (150g)"
  - "2 garlic cloves, minced"
  - "2 pounds chicken thighs, skinless (900g)"
  - "1 teaspoon ground cumin"
  - "1 teaspoon ground cinnamon"
  - "1 teaspoon ground ginger"
  - "1 teaspoon paprika"
  - "1 cup chicken broth (240ml)"
  - "1 cup dried apricots, chopped (150g)"
  - "1/2 cup slivered almonds (75g)"
  - "1 cup canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed (240g)"
  - "Salt and black pepper to taste"
  - "Fresh cilantro, for garnish"
steps:
  - "Heat the olive oil in a large skillet or tagine over medium heat."
  - "Add the chopped onion and minced garlic; sauté until translucent, about 5 minutes."
  - "Season the chicken thighs with salt and pepper, then add them to the pan."
  - "Brown the chicken on both sides, about 5 minutes per side."
  - "Stir in the cumin, cinnamon, ginger, and paprika; cook for 1 minute until fragrant."
  - "Pour in the chicken broth and add the chopped apricots; bring to a simmer."
  - "Cover and cook on low heat for 30 minutes, until chicken is cooked through."
  - "Add the chickpeas and slivered almonds; cook for an additional 5 minutes."
  - "Taste and adjust seasoning, if necessary."
  - "Garnish with fresh cilantro before serving."
tips:
  - "Serve with couscous or rice to soak up the delicious sauce."
  - "Adjust the spices according to your taste; add cayenne for heat."
  - "Use leftover tagine as a filling for wraps or as a topping for salads."

## Discover the Flavors of Morocco

If you're looking to bring a taste of Morocco into your kitchen, this easy chicken tagine with apricots and almonds is the perfect dish to try. It combines tender chicken with the sweetness of apricots and the crunch of almonds, creating a delightful balance of flavors that will impress your family and friends. Best of all, it can be made in just an hour!

## A One-Pan Wonder

The beauty of this Moroccan tagine is that it's a one-pan dish, allowing for easy prep and cleanup. Utilizing common ingredients found in your local supermarket, this dish emphasizes the aromatic spices that define Moroccan cuisine. From cumin to cinnamon, each spice adds a depth that transports your taste buds to a bustling market in Marrakech.

## Serve and Enjoy

Serve your tagine over fluffy couscous or rice to enjoy every drop of the savory sauce. Whether it's a weeknight dinner or a festive gathering, this Moroccan chicken tagine is sure to be a hit. Don’t forget to garnish with fresh cilantro for a pop of color and freshness!

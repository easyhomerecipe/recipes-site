ingredients:
  - "1 cup (185 g) quinoa, rinsed"
  - "2 cups (480 ml) vegetable broth"
  - "1 can (15 oz / 425 g) black beans, drained and rinsed"
  - "1 cup (150 g) cherry tomatoes, halved"
  - "1 cup (150 g) corn, frozen or fresh"
  - "1 ripe avocado, diced"
  - "1/4 cup (60 ml) lime juice"
  - "1 tablespoon (15 ml) olive oil"
  - "1/4 teaspoon cumin"
  - "Salt and pepper to taste"
  - "1/4 cup (15 g) chopped fresh cilantro"
steps:
  - "In a medium saucepan, combine quinoa and vegetable broth."
  - "Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes."
  - "Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes."
  - "Fluff quinoa with a fork and allow to cool slightly."
  - "In a large bowl, combine the cooled quinoa, black beans, cherry tomatoes, corn, and avocado."
  - "In a small bowl, whisk together lime juice, olive oil, cumin, salt, and pepper."
  - "Pour the dressing over the salad and toss gently to combine."
  - "Garnish with chopped cilantro before serving."

## A Fresh and Healthy Choice
Quinoa salad is a fantastic option when you're looking for something nutritious and filling. Packed with protein and fiber, this zesty quinoa salad with black beans and avocado is not only delightful to eat but also brings a burst of color to your table. It's perfect as a side dish or even a light meal on its own. The combination of textures—from the fluffiness of quinoa to the creaminess of avocado—creates a satisfying bite.

## Versatile and Customizable
The beauty of this salad is its versatility. You can easily add or swap ingredients based on what you have on hand. Want to throw in some bell peppers or cucumbers? Go for it! Each variation can give this dish a whole new life while keeping it healthy and delicious. Additionally, this recipe is vegan-friendly, making it a great choice for gatherings where dietary restrictions may be a concern. 

## Meal Prep Wonder
Another great aspect of this quinoa salad is that it holds up well in the fridge. Perfect for meal prep, you can make a large batch and enjoy it throughout the week. Pack it for lunch or serve it at a picnic—it's a crowd-pleaser that everyone will love.

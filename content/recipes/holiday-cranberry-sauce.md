ingredients:
  - "12 ounces (340 g) fresh or frozen cranberries"
  - "1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar"
  - "1 cup (240 ml) water"
  - "1/2 cup (120 ml) orange juice"
  - "1 teaspoon (5 g) orange zest"
  - "1/2 teaspoon (2 g) ground cinnamon"
  - "1/4 teaspoon (1 g) ground nutmeg"
  - "1/4 teaspoon (1 g) salt"
steps:
  - "In a medium saucepan, combine water and sugar over medium heat."
  - "Stir until sugar is dissolved, about 2-3 minutes."
  - "Add cranberries, orange juice, orange zest, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt to the saucepan."
  - "Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low."
  - "Simmer for 10-12 minutes until cranberries burst and sauce thickens."
  - "Remove from heat and let cool for at least 30 minutes."
  - "Transfer the sauce to a bowl and refrigerate until ready to serve."

## Introduction to Holiday Cranberry Sauce
Cranberry sauce is a classic holiday dish that adds a burst of flavor to your festive meals. It's not only a beautiful addition to your table, but it's also surprisingly easy to make. This homemade cranberry sauce is tart, sweet, and aromatic, capturing the essence of the holiday season in every spoonful.

## Why You Should Make Your Own
Store-bought cranberry sauces often lack the fresh taste and depth of flavor that homemade versions provide. By making your own, you can customize the sweetness, add spices, and even throw in other flavors like citrus or nuts. Plus, it makes your kitchen smell wonderful while it cooks!

## Serving Suggestions
This cranberry sauce pairs perfectly with turkey, ham, or even as a spread on sandwiches. It’s also fantastic over pancakes or waffles for a festive breakfast. Make sure to prepare it ahead of time to let the flavors meld, and you’ll have a delicious side dish that everyone will love!

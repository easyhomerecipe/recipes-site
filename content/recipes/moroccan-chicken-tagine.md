ingredients:
  - "1 whole chicken (about 1.5 kg), cut into 8 pieces"
  - "2 large onions, grated"
  - "4 garlic cloves, minced"
  - "1 tsp ground ginger"
  - "1 tsp ground turmeric"
  - "1/2 tsp saffron threads, soaked in 2 tbsp warm water"
  - "1 tsp black pepper"
  - "1 tsp salt"
  - "1/4 cup olive oil"
  - "1 small bunch fresh cilantro and parsley, chopped"
  - "1 preserved lemon, rind cut into strips"
  - "1 cup green olives"
  - "1 cup water"
steps:
  - "In a large bowl, mix the grated onion, garlic, ginger, turmeric, saffron, pepper, salt, olive oil and half the herbs. Add the chicken and coat well. Marinate for at least 30 minutes (or overnight in the fridge)."
  - "Place the chicken and marinade in a tagine or heavy pot over medium heat. Cook for 10 minutes, turning the pieces until lightly golden."
  - "Add the water, cover and simmer on low heat for 40 minutes, until the chicken is tender."
  - "Add the preserved lemon strips and olives. Cook uncovered for 10 more minutes until the sauce thickens."
  - "Sprinkle with the remaining herbs and serve hot with Moroccan bread (khobz)."
tips:
  - "Rinse the olives if they are very salty, and taste before adding extra salt."
  - "No tagine? A Dutch oven or heavy pot works perfectly."
faq:
  - q: "Can I make chicken tagine without a tagine pot?"
    a: "Yes. Use any heavy pot with a tight lid, such as a Dutch oven. Keep the heat low so the sauce does not burn."
  - q: "What can I use instead of preserved lemons?"
    a: "Use the zest of one fresh lemon plus 1 tablespoon of lemon juice, added at the end of cooking."
  - q: "What do you serve with chicken tagine?"
    a: "Traditionally it is eaten with Moroccan bread to scoop up the sauce. Couscous or rice also work well."

Chicken tagine with preserved lemons and olives is one of the most loved dishes of Moroccan home cooking. The chicken slowly cooks in a fragrant sauce of onions, ginger, turmeric and saffron until it becomes incredibly tender.

## Why you will love this recipe

- **Simple ingredients** you can find in any supermarket.
- **One pot** – less washing up.
- **Make-ahead friendly** – it tastes even better the next day.

## Key ingredients

**Preserved lemons** give the tagine its unique tangy flavor. **Saffron** adds color and aroma, and **green olives** balance the sauce with a light saltiness.

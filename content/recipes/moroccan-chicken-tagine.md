ingredients:
  - "2 lbs (900 g) chicken thighs, bone-in and skinless"
  - "2 tbsp (30 ml) olive oil"
  - "1 large onion, finely chopped"
  - "3 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "1 tsp (2 g) ground ginger"
  - "1 tsp (2 g) ground cumin"
  - "1 tsp (2 g) ground cinnamon"
  - "1/2 tsp (1 g) ground turmeric"
  - "1/2 tsp (1 g) black pepper"
  - "1/2 tsp (1 g) salt"
  - "1 1/2 cups (360 ml) chicken broth"
  - "1/2 cup (120 g) green olives, pitted and halved"
  - "1 preserved lemon, peeled and thinly sliced"
  - "Fresh cilantro, for garnish"
steps:
  - "In a large tagine or heavy pot, heat olive oil over medium heat."
  - "Add chopped onion and garlic; sauté until soft, about 5 minutes."
  - "In a bowl, mix together ginger, cumin, cinnamon, turmeric, black pepper, and salt."
  - "Add spice mixture to the pot and stir for 1-2 minutes until fragrant."
  - "Add chicken thighs to the pot, browning on all sides for about 5-7 minutes."
  - "Pour in the chicken broth, bringing the mixture to a gentle simmer."
  - "Cover and cook on low heat for 40 minutes, stirring occasionally."
  - "Add olives and preserved lemons; cook uncovered for an additional 10-15 minutes."
  - "Check chicken is cooked to 165°F (74°C); remove from heat."
  - "Garnish with fresh cilantro before serving."
tips:
  - "For the best flavor, marinate the chicken in spices for a few hours or overnight."
  - "Serve with warm bread or couscous to soak up the delicious sauce."

## Discover the Rich Flavors of Moroccan Cuisine

Moroccan cuisine is known for its unique blend of spices and flavors, making dishes like Chicken Tagine with Preserved Lemons and Olives truly delightful. This dish is not only aromatic and flavorful but also easy to prepare, making it perfect for a cozy family dinner or an elegant gathering. 

## A Culinary Tradition

Tagine refers to both the cooking vessel and the method of cooking that brings out the best flavors in the ingredients. In this recipe, we're using chicken thighs, which stay juicy and tender as they cook slowly with spices. The addition of preserved lemons and olives not only enhances the flavor but also adds a zesty, tangy touch that is signature to Moroccan dishes.  

## Enjoying Your Tagine

After cooking, don't forget to garnish your dish with fresh cilantro for a pop of color and added freshness. Pair it with fluffy couscous or fresh bread to soak up the delicious sauce. This Moroccan Chicken Tagine is sure to become a favorite in your household, and you might even find that it tastes better the next day as the flavors continue to meld together!

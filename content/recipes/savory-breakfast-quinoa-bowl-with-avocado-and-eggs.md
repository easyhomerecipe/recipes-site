ingredients:
  - "1 cup quinoa (170g), rinsed"
  - "2 cups water (480ml)"
  - "2 large eggs"
  - "1 ripe avocado, sliced"
  - "1/2 cup cherry tomatoes (75g), halved"
  - "1/4 cup green onion (25g), chopped"
  - "1 tablespoon olive oil (15ml)"
  - "Salt and pepper, to taste"
  - "1/2 teaspoon garlic powder"
  - "1/4 teaspoon paprika"
  - "Fresh cilantro, for garnish (optional)"
steps:
  - "In a medium saucepan, combine quinoa and water; bring to a boil."
  - "Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer until quinoa is tender, about 15 minutes."
  - "While the quinoa cooks, bring a small pot of water to a boil for the eggs."
  - "Once boiling, carefully add eggs and boil for 6-7 minutes for soft-boiled."
  - "Remove the eggs and place them in ice water for a few minutes to stop cooking."
  - "In a large bowl, combine cooked quinoa, olive oil, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper."
  - "Divide the quinoa mixture into bowls and top with sliced avocado, cherry tomatoes, and green onion."
  - "Peel the eggs, slice them in half, and place on top of the bowls."
  - "Garnish with fresh cilantro if desired, and serve immediately."
tips:
  - "For added flavor, try adding a squeeze of lime juice over the avocado."

### Start Your Day with a Healthy Twist

Breakfast is the most important meal of the day, and it doesn't have to be all about pancakes and syrup! This Savory Breakfast Quinoa Bowl with Avocado and Eggs is a nutritious way to kick off your morning. Packed with protein, healthy fats, and fiber, this dish will keep you satisfied until lunchtime.

### Why Quinoa?

Quinoa is not only gluten-free but also a complete protein, making it an excellent choice for breakfast. Unlike other grains, it contains all nine essential amino acids. Combined with creamy avocado and perfectly boiled eggs, this meal provides a balanced start to your day. The cherry tomatoes and green onions add a fresh pop of flavor, while spices like garlic powder and paprika give it a delightful kick.

### Quick and Easy to Prepare

This breakfast bowl can be made in just about 25 minutes, making it perfect for busy mornings or those lazy weekend brunches. You can even prepare the quinoa ahead of time and quickly assemble your bowl in the morning for an extra fast breakfast option. Enjoy a wholesome and satisfying meal that is both colorful and delicious!

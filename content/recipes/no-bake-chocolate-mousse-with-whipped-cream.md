ingredients:
  - "1 cup (240 ml) heavy cream"
  - "7 oz (200 g) semi-sweet chocolate, chopped"
  - "3 large egg yolks"
  - "2 tablespoons (25 g) granulated sugar"
  - "1 teaspoon vanilla extract"
  - "1/4 teaspoon salt"
  - "1/2 cup (120 ml) milk"
  - "1 cup (240 ml) additional heavy cream for whipped topping"
  - "2 tablespoons powdered sugar for whipped topping"
  - "Chocolate shavings for garnish (optional)"
steps:
  - "In a medium bowl, melt the chopped chocolate over a double boiler or in the microwave, stirring until smooth."
  - "In another bowl, whisk together the egg yolks, granulated sugar, vanilla extract, and salt until combined."
  - "Heat the milk until steaming, then slowly whisk it into the egg yolk mixture. Pour everything back into the saucepan and cook over low heat, stirring constantly, until it thickens slightly and reaches 160°F (71°C) on a thermometer, about 3-5 minutes."
  - "Gradually add the melted chocolate to the egg mixture, stirring until fully combined."
  - "In a separate bowl, whip 1 cup of heavy cream until stiff peaks form."
  - "Gently fold the whipped cream into the chocolate mixture until no white streaks remain."
  - "Spoon the mousse into serving dishes and refrigerate for at least 2 hours until set."
  - "Before serving, whip the additional heavy cream with powdered sugar until stiff peaks form."
  - "Top each mousse with a dollop of whipped cream and garnish with chocolate shavings if desired."
tips:
  - "Cooking the egg yolks to 160°F (71°C) makes them safe to eat. You can also use pasteurized eggs."
  - "Make sure to use quality chocolate for the best flavor."
  - "Be gentle when folding in the whipped cream to keep the mousse light and airy."
  - "Refrigerating for longer than 2 hours will enhance the flavors and texture."

## The Perfect No-Bake Dessert

If you’re looking for a simple yet indulgent dessert, this no-bake chocolate mousse is the answer! With just a few ingredients, you can create a rich and creamy treat that will wow your family and friends. The good news is that you don’t need to bake anything, making it a perfect option for warm days or when you want a quick dessert.

## Ingredients You’ll Love

This recipe uses semi-sweet chocolate as its base, paired with heavy cream to give it that velvety texture we all love. The addition of egg yolks creates a luxurious richness while the whipped cream adds lightness to the dish. You’ll also find some vanilla and a pinch of salt to enhance the chocolate flavor.

## Easy to Prepare

Making the chocolate mousse is as easy as melting chocolate, combining it with egg yolks, and folding in whipped cream. It requires minimal effort and can be prepared in just 15 minutes! Just be sure to let it chill for a couple of hours before serving to allow the flavors to develop. Whether it’s for a fancy dinner party or a simple weeknight treat, this no-bake chocolate mousse will surely satisfy any sweet tooth.

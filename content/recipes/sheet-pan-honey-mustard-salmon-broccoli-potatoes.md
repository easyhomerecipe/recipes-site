servings: 4

ingredients:
  - "4 (5 oz) salmon fillets (140 g each)"
  - "2 cups broccoli florets (150 g)"
  - "1 lb baby potatoes, halved (450 g)"
  - "2 tbsp olive oil (30 ml)"
  - "3 tbsp honey (60 g)"
  - "2 tbsp Dijon mustard (30 g)"
  - "1 tsp garlic powder (4 g)"
  - "1 tsp paprika (3 g)"
  - "1/2 tsp salt (3 g)"
  - "1/4 tsp black pepper (1 g)"
  - "1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped (optional)"
steps:
  - "Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C)."
  - "In a large bowl, mix olive oil, honey, Dijon mustard, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and black pepper."
  - "Add the halved baby potatoes to the bowl and toss to coat them well with the mixture."
  - "Spread the potatoes evenly on a sheet pan and roast for 15 minutes."
  - "While the potatoes are roasting, line another part of the sheet pan with broccoli florets."
  - "Place the salmon fillets on the pan with the potatoes and broccoli."
  - "Drizzle the remaining honey mustard mixture over the salmon and broccoli."
  - "Return the pan to the oven and bake for an additional 10-12 minutes, or until the salmon is cooked through."
  - "Garnish with fresh parsley if desired and serve warm."
tips:
  - "Use a meat thermometer to check that the salmon reaches an internal temperature of 145°F (63°C)."
  - "Swap out broccoli for any seasonal vegetables like asparagus or green beans."
  - "For extra crispy potatoes, cut them into smaller pieces or parboil them for a few minutes before roasting."

## A Simple and Healthy Dinner Option

If you’re looking for a quick and nutritious dinner that the whole family will love, this sheet-pan honey mustard salmon with broccoli and potatoes is a great choice. Not only is it delicious, but it also makes for easy cleanup since everything cooks on one pan. The savory mixture of honey and Dijon mustard creates a delightful glaze for the salmon, while the crispy baby potatoes and vibrant broccoli add texture and nutrients.

## Cooking Made Easy

Sheet-pan meals are a fantastic way to simplify weeknight dinners. You can prep everything in just 15 minutes, allowing you to spend more time enjoying your meal rather than prepping and cleaning. The combination of protein from the salmon and fiber from the veggies makes this dish well-balanced. Plus, the kids will love the sweet honey mustard flavor! 

## Perfect for Meal Prep

This recipe is also great for meal prepping. You can double the ingredients and pack up the leftovers for lunch the next day. With minimal effort, you can enjoy a healthy and satisfying meal any time you need it. So the next time you're in a rush, remember this recipe for a wholesome dinner straight from the oven to your table.

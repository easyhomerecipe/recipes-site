ingredients:
  - "1 cup (200 g) green or brown lentils, rinsed"
  - "1 medium onion, diced"
  - "2 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "1 medium carrot, diced"
  - "1 medium bell pepper, chopped"
  - "1 medium zucchini, diced"
  - "1 can (14 oz or 400 g) diced tomatoes"
  - "4 cups (960 ml) vegetable broth"
  - "2 tablespoons olive oil"
  - "1 tablespoon ground cumin"
  - "1 tablespoon paprika"
  - "1 teaspoon ground turmeric"
  - "1 teaspoon cinnamon"
  - "Salt and pepper to taste"
  - "Fresh parsley for garnish"
steps:
  - "In a large pot, heat olive oil over medium heat."
  - "Add diced onion and sauté until translucent, about 5 minutes."
  - "Stir in minced garlic, cumin, paprika, turmeric, and cinnamon; cook for 1 minute until fragrant."
  - "Add diced carrot, bell pepper, and zucchini to the pot and sauté for another 5 minutes."
  - "Pour in the diced tomatoes and vegetable broth; stir to combine."
  - "Add the rinsed lentils; bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce to a simmer."
  - "Cover the pot and let it simmer for 25-30 minutes, or until lentils are tender."
  - "Season with salt and pepper to taste before serving."
  - "Garnish with freshly chopped parsley and enjoy!"
tips:
  - "For a spicier kick, add a pinch of cayenne pepper to the spice mix."
  - "You can substitute any seasonal vegetables you have on hand."
  - "Leftovers can be refrigerated and taste even better the next day!"

## A Hearty and Flavorful Stew
Lentil stew is a wonderful dish that warms the soul. Packed with nutrients, this Moroccan spiced lentil stew is perfect for a cozy night in or when you want to impress guests with your cooking skills. Bursting with flavor from spices such as cumin and cinnamon, this dish is not only healthy but also incredibly satisfying.

## Simple Ingredients, Big Flavor
You'll find that the ingredients for this stew are quite simple and mostly pantry staples. Lentils form the protein base of the dish, while the addition of fresh vegetables provides texture and color. The spices are what truly elevate this dish, infusing it with that signature Moroccan flair we all love. 

## Cooking Tips and Variations
Whether you are a seasoned cook or a novice, this recipe is straightforward, making it an ideal candidate for weeknight dinners. Feel free to customize the vegetables according to what's in season or even what you have left in your fridge. And don't forget, leftovers are a treat in themselves because they allow all the delicious flavors to meld together even more!
Enjoy this savory Moroccan spiced lentil stew as a delightful and filling meal any day of the week.

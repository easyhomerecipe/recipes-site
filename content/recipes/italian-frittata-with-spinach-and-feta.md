ingredients:
  - "8 large eggs"
  - "1 cup (150g) fresh spinach, chopped"
  - "1/2 cup (75g) crumbled feta cheese"
  - "1/4 cup (60ml) milk"
  - "1 small onion, diced"
  - "1 clove garlic, minced"
  - "2 tablespoons (30ml) olive oil"
  - "Salt and pepper, to taste"
  - "1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)"
steps:
  - "Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C)."
  - "In a large bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, salt, and pepper until well combined."
  - "Heat the olive oil in a large, oven-safe skillet over medium heat."
  - "Add the diced onion and sauté until translucent, about 3-4 minutes."
  - "Stir in the minced garlic and chopped spinach, cooking until the spinach wilts, about 2 minutes."
  - "Pour the egg mixture into the skillet, and sprinkle the crumbled feta on top."
  - "Cook undisturbed for 2-3 minutes, allowing the edges to set."
  - "Transfer the skillet to the preheated oven and bake for 10-12 minutes, or until the frittata is set and slightly golden."
  - "Remove from the oven and let cool for a few minutes before slicing."
  - "Serve warm or at room temperature."
tips:
  - "For added flavor, include other vegetables like bell peppers or mushrooms."
  - "Use a non-stick skillet for easier removal and cleanup."
  - "This frittata can be made ahead and stored in the fridge for quick breakfasts."

### A Delicious Start to Your Day
If you're looking for a breakfast that's both quick and satisfying, this Italian frittata with spinach and feta is sure to impress. This dish is not only packed with nutrients but also full of flavor, making it a favorite among families and individuals alike. Frittatas are versatile, allowing you to customize them with your choice of vegetables and cheese, but the combination of spinach and feta is a classic that never disappoints.

### Simple Ingredients for a Tasty Meal
At its core, a frittata is made with eggs, and you’ll find that the ingredients for this dish are simple and easily found in your kitchen or local supermarket. Fresh spinach brings a mild earthiness, while the feta cheese adds a tangy burst of flavor. Plus, the inclusion of garlic and onion elevates this dish to new culinary heights.

### Cooking and Serving
What sets this frittata apart is its ease of preparation. You'll start by sautéing the onion and garlic, then mix in the spinach, followed by the egg mixture. A quick time in the oven allows it to puff up beautifully. This dish can be served warm or at room temperature, making it perfect for breakfast, brunch, or even a light dinner. Give this frittata a try, and enjoy a nutritious meal that can be made in no time!

ingredients:
  - "4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 680g)"
  - "2 tablespoons olive oil (30ml)"
  - "2 lemons (juice and zest)"
  - "4 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "1 teaspoon dried oregano (1g)"
  - "1 teaspoon dried thyme (1g)"
  - "Salt and pepper to taste"
  - "2 cups broccoli florets (150g)"
  - "1 cup cherry tomatoes (150g)"
  - "1 bell pepper, chopped"
  - "Fresh parsley for garnish"
steps:
  - "Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C)."
  - "In a small bowl, mix olive oil, lemon juice, lemon zest, garlic, oregano, thyme, salt, and pepper."
  - "Place chicken breasts in a baking dish and pour the herb mixture over them."
  - "Add broccoli, cherry tomatoes, and bell pepper around the chicken."
  - "Toss the vegetables gently in the remaining marinade."
  - "Bake for 25-30 minutes, or until the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C)."
  - "Remove from the oven and let rest for 5 minutes."
  - "Garnish with fresh parsley before serving."
tips:

## Introduction
Baked lemon herb chicken with veggies is not only a delicious dinner option but also a healthy one that your family will love. Perfectly seasoned chicken breasts are paired with vibrant vegetables, making this dish a colorful and nutritious meal. In just under an hour, you’ll have a complete dinner on the table that’s both easy to prepare and satisfying.

## Simple Preparation
This recipe requires only a handful of ingredients and minimal prep time, making it ideal for busy weeknights. Begin by marinating the chicken in a zesty lemon-herb mixture. While the chicken absorbs those lovely flavors, you can chop up your favorite veggies. The beauty of this dish is its versatility; you can use whatever seasonal vegetables you have on hand. Broccoli, bell peppers, and cherry tomatoes work beautifully, but feel free to experiment!

## Cooking and Serving
Once everything is prepared, pop it into the oven. The chicken will cook to juicy perfection while the vegetables roast alongside it, soaking up all those tasty juices. Once it’s done, a sprinkle of fresh parsley adds a nice finishing touch. Serve this dish with a side of whole grains for a balanced meal that’s rich in flavor and nutrients. Enjoy your healthy homemade dinner effortlessly!

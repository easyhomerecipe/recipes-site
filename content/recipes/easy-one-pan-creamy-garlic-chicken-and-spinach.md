ingredients:
  - "4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 680g)"
  - "2 tablespoons olive oil (30ml)"
  - "4 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "1 cup chicken broth (240ml)"
  - "1 cup heavy cream (240ml)"
  - "1 cup fresh spinach (30g)"
  - "1 teaspoon Italian seasoning (1g)"
  - "1/2 teaspoon salt (3g)"
  - "1/4 teaspoon black pepper (1g)"
  - "1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese (50g)"
  - "Fresh parsley for garnish (optional)"
steps:
  - "Season the chicken breasts with salt and pepper."
  - "Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat."
  - "Add the chicken breasts and cook for 6-7 minutes on each side until golden brown and cooked through."
  - "Remove the chicken from the skillet and set aside."
  - "In the same skillet, add minced garlic and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant."
  - "Pour in the chicken broth and heavy cream, stirring to combine."
  - "Add the Italian seasoning and bring the mixture to a simmer."
  - "Stir in the fresh spinach and let it wilt for about 2 minutes."
  - "Return the chicken to the skillet and sprinkle with Parmesan cheese."
  - "Simmer for an additional 2-3 minutes until the cheese melts, then garnish with parsley if desired."
tips:
  - "For a lighter version, substitute heavy cream with half-and-half."
  - "Serve this dish with pasta or rice to soak up the creamy sauce."
  - "Make sure the chicken is cooked through to an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C)."

ingredients:
  - "2 tablespoons olive oil (30 ml)"
  - "1 large onion, chopped (150 g)"
  - "2 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "1 medium carrot, diced (120 g)"
  - "1 medium zucchini, diced (200 g)"
  - "1 red bell pepper, diced (150 g)"
  - "1 cup canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed (240 g)"
  - "1 can diced tomatoes (400 g)"
  - "1 tablespoon ground cumin"
  - "1 tablespoon ground coriander"
  - "1 teaspoon ground cinnamon"
  - "1 teaspoon paprika"
  - "Salt and pepper to taste"
  - "Fresh cilantro or parsley for garnish"
steps:
  - "Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat."
  - "Add the chopped onion and cook until softened, about 5 minutes."
  - "Stir in the minced garlic and cook for an additional minute."
  - "Add the diced carrot, zucchini, and red bell pepper, cooking for about 5-7 minutes until vegetables start to soften."
  - "Mix in the chickpeas, diced tomatoes, cumin, coriander, cinnamon, and paprika."
  - "Season with salt and pepper, then bring the mixture to a simmer."
  - "Cover and cook for 25-30 minutes, stirring occasionally until the vegetables are tender."
  - "Remove from heat and let sit for a few minutes before serving."
  - "Garnish with fresh cilantro or parsley before serving."
tips:
  - "You can add any seasonal vegetables you have on hand for extra flavor."
  - "Serve with couscous or crusty bread to soak up the delicious sauce."
  - "For added spice, incorporate some chili flakes or harissa into the dish."

## Discover the Flavor of Moroccan Cuisine
If you're looking for a wholesome and comforting dish, this Moroccan Vegetable Tagine is the perfect choice. Bursting with vibrant colors and rich flavors, this dish combines a variety of vegetables with aromatic spices that are characteristic of Moroccan cuisine. Plus, it is simple enough for busy weeknights and suitable for both vegetarians and meat-lovers alike.

## Ingredients That Shine
The beauty of a tagine lies in its versatility. You can easily adapt this recipe depending on what vegetables are in season or what you have in your pantry. This version features a medley of carrots, zucchini, and bell peppers, along with protein-rich chickpeas. The spices give the dish its authentic Moroccan flavor, making it not just a meal but a delightful culinary experience.

## Serving Suggestions
Serve this flavorful tagine hot, garnished with fresh herbs. It goes beautifully with fluffy couscous or warm crusty bread, allowing you to savor every drop of the delicious sauce. Whether it’s a family dinner or a meal prep for the week, this vegetable tagine will not disappoint. Enjoy a taste of Morocco in your own kitchen!

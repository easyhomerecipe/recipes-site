ingredients:
  - "1 cup (125g) all-purpose flour"
  - "2 tbsp (25g) sugar"
  - "1 tsp baking powder"
  - "1/2 tsp baking soda"
  - "1/4 tsp salt"
  - "1 cup (240ml) buttermilk"
  - "1 large egg"
  - "2 tbsp (30g) melted butter"
  - "1 cup (150g) fresh blueberries"
  - "Butter or oil for cooking"
steps:
  - "In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt."
  - "In another bowl, mix the buttermilk, egg, and melted butter until well combined."
  - "Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix gently until just combined; the batter will be lumpy."
  - "Fold in the blueberries carefully so they don't break."
  - "Preheat a non-stick skillet over medium heat and add a little butter or oil."
  - "Pour about 1/4 cup of batter for each pancake onto the skillet."
  - "Cook until bubbles form on the surface, about 2-3 minutes, then flip and cook for another 2 minutes or until golden brown."
  - "Repeat with the remaining batter, adding more butter or oil to the skillet as needed."
  - "Serve warm with maple syrup, additional blueberries, or your favorite toppings."
  - "Enjoy your delicious pancakes!"
tips:
  - "Use fresh blueberries for the best flavor; frozen can be used but may make the batter slightly watery."
  - "For fluffier pancakes, do not overmix the batter; it's okay if there are some lumps."
  - "Keep pancakes warm in a low oven (around 200°F/95°C) while you finish cooking the rest."

## Start Your Morning Right with Homemade Blueberry Pancakes
There's nothing better than waking up to a stack of warm, fluffy pancakes, especially when they're bursting with juicy blueberries. These easy homemade blueberry pancakes are perfect for a cozy weekend breakfast or even a quick weekday treat. With just a few simple ingredients, you'll have a delicious meal that the whole family will love!

## Why Blueberry Pancakes?
Blueberries not only add a sweet and tangy flavor to pancakes, but they're also packed with antioxidants and nutrients. These pancakes are versatile too! You can top them with maple syrup, whipped cream, or even a sprinkle of powdered sugar. They can also be easily modified to suit your dietary preferences, such as using whole wheat flour or a dairy-free milk substitute.

## Tips for Perfect Pancakes
For the fluffiest pancakes, it’s important not to overmix the batter. A few lumps are totally fine! Also, keep your skillet at a medium heat; too high and the pancakes will burn before they’re cooked through. And most importantly, enjoy cooking them as much as you enjoy eating them!
